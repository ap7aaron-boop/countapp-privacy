Privacy Policy

CountApp Arcade · Package com.naarodev.countapp · Last updated 30 September 2026

CountApp Arcade can be played without an account. Core game progress is stored locally on the user's device. When optional Google Sign-In and cloud backup are enabled and chosen by the user, limited account and game-save data is transmitted to CountApp Arcade's backend to provide those features. CountApp Arcade does not sell personal information and does not use advertising or behavioural-tracking SDKs. The current Android app does not include online account, cloud-save or ranking features; it stores all progress on the device and sends no personal data.

1. Local game data

CountApp Arcade stores game data locally so progress is available when the player returns. This may include active-play time, points, unlocks, purchases made with in-game points, achievements, milestones, statistics, high scores, collectible progress, home and decoration layouts, pet state, audio settings, accessibility settings, onboarding state, and other game preferences.

Local game data remains on the device unless the user explicitly uses an online feature such as cloud backup. CountApp Arcade does not award offline progress.

2. Optional Google Sign-In and online account data

If a user chooses Google Sign-In, CountApp Arcade may receive and store the Google account identifier required to identify the CountApp Arcade account, along with the email address, display name, and profile image URL supplied by Google for that sign-in. This information is used for account management, authentication, and user-facing account identification.

CountApp Arcade does not request access to Google contacts, Google Drive files, precise location, health data, financial data, or other unrelated Google account information for normal account use.

3. Optional cloud-save data

If the user chooses to back up progress, CountApp Arcade transmits the current legitimate CountApp Arcade save to the CountApp Arcade backend. The cloud save can include gameplay progress, points, unlocks, achievements, statistics, high scores, owned items, collectibles, home layouts, pet state, settings, and other fields contained in the local CountApp Arcade save.

Cloud backup and restore are optional. A player can continue using CountApp Arcade without creating an online account.

4. Public rankings and shared content

On hosted builds with online services enabled, players can browse global arcade rankings. Sharing a score requires Google Sign-In and a separate, optional sharing choice in Global rankings. CountApp publishes a generated player alias, best score and rank for each game and mode. It does not publish the player's Google name, email address, profile image, or Google account identifier.

When sharing is enabled, future best runs are queued on the device and sent when a connection and a valid account session are available. A submission contains the game and mode, score and active run duration; the backend stores the generated alias, best scores and an update timestamp linked privately to the account. Test Mode results and imported saved records are not submitted. Turning sharing off stops new submissions and clears unsent scores. Published scores remain until account deletion. Daily challenge boards close after one year, and old daily records are removed when that account next submits a score. Other best scores remain with the account. Shared homes are not currently available.

5. Information CountApp Arcade does not currently collect for advertising

CountApp Arcade does not currently include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioural advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs used to build advertising profiles. CountApp Arcade does not sell personal data.

6. Network and hosting information

CountApp Arcade's game artwork and runtime assets are bundled with the application. Online account and cloud-backup features use CountApp Arcade's backend hosted on Google Cloud when those features are enabled. Standard hosting infrastructure may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as IP address, request time, requested resource, browser or user-agent information, and security or diagnostic logs.

Technical information processed by Google Cloud is subject to the provider's applicable security, operational, and privacy practices. CountApp Arcade does not use hosting logs to build advertising profiles or track players across unrelated apps or websites.

7. How information is used

Local game data is used to save progress, restore settings, track achievements and statistics, maintain owned items and collectibles, and preserve game state. Optional online account information is used for authentication and account management. Optional cloud-save data is used only to provide backup and restore functionality and related online CountApp Arcade features.

8. Sharing and service providers

CountApp Arcade does not sell personal data. CountApp Arcade does not intentionally share gameplay save data with advertisers or data brokers. Google and Google Cloud may process information as service providers when supplying Google Sign-In, hosting, authentication support, storage, security, and related infrastructure needed to operate optional online features.

9. Security and encryption

Online CountApp Arcade traffic is intended to be delivered over HTTPS/TLS. Google Sign-In credentials are verified by the CountApp Arcade backend before account data is accepted. Account-related cookies used by the hosted service are configured as secure, HTTP-only cookies. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, but CountApp Arcade is designed to minimize collection and keep online features optional.

10. Data retention and local deletion

Locally stored CountApp Arcade data remains on the device until it is cleared through available game or device controls, the application's storage is cleared, or the application is uninstalled, subject to device or browser storage behaviour. Any save backup deliberately exported by the user is controlled by the user after export.

Online profile and cloud-save records remain until the user deletes the online CountApp Arcade account or the records are otherwise removed as part of service maintenance. Standard infrastructure/security logs may be retained according to the hosting provider's configured operational retention settings.

11. Account deletion

Users with an online CountApp Arcade account can request permanent account deletion from the in-app Account panel or from the CountApp Arcade account deletion page, which will be linked here when online accounts are available.

Successful account deletion removes CountApp Arcade's online profile, cloud backup, published rankings and any account-associated public-home record. If deletion cannot be confirmed, the user can retry. CountApp Arcade does not intentionally retain those account records after successful deletion. Local progress on the user's device is separate and can be removed through CountApp Arcade's local reset controls, application-storage controls, or uninstalling the application.

12. Children's privacy

CountApp Arcade does not intentionally require children to provide personal information to play the core game. If a future version is directed to children or materially changes its account, social, advertising, or data practices, the applicable child-safety and privacy requirements must be reviewed before release.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CountApp Arcade's features or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made. The policy and Google Play Data safety disclosures should be updated before releasing a version that changes how user or device data is handled.

14. Privacy contact

For privacy questions about CountApp Arcade, use the developer contact email shown on CountApp Arcade's Google Play store listing. Please include "CountApp Arcade privacy" in the subject of your message.
